# Descent transition video

`public/assets/video/descent.mp4` bridges the visual-novel intro into the live workbench.

- Generated by [make-descent.py](make-descent.py) (Pillow + ffmpeg/libx264): 1024×1536 (the plate's native size), 30 fps, 6 s, yuv420p, CRF 29, `+faststart`, no audio track.
- Sources: the shipped plate `public/assets/textures/workshop-v4.webp`, and [game-first-frame.png](game-first-frame.png). That frame was captured from the running game at 1024×1536 in its first state (the press empty, all three lots on the trays, as PRD 1.2 lets the player choose) with the HUD hidden, so the last shot matches what the player sees next.
- Shot: porthole close-up with rising bubbles, lights flicker on while the camera pulls back to the whole workshop, a dip to dark, then the real first frame.
- No new generated imagery. Regenerate with `python3 assets/source/video/make-descent.py` after the plate or the first frame changes.
